Data Deletion

Pixora PDF has no accounts and keeps no data on any server, so you stay in full control of your files.

No account, no server-side data

We do not collect or store personal data on our servers, so there is no account or online data for us to delete. Everything the app creates is on your device.

Delete your PDFs

  1. Open Pixora PDF and go to the Files tab.
  2. Tap the ⋮ menu next to a PDF and choose Delete.

You can also delete files in your Files app from Downloads/Pixora PDF or Documents/Pixora PDF, and images created by “PDF to Images” from Pictures/Pixora PDF.

Delete app settings and cache

Go to Android Settings → Apps → Pixora PDF → Storage and choose Clear storage, or uninstall the app. This removes preferences, favourites and temporary scan photos.

After uninstalling

Android may keep PDFs you saved in Downloads or Documents. Delete them manually if you no longer want them.
