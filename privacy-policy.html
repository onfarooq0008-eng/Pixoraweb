Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 20, 2026. Short version: Pixora PDF does not collect, store on a server, or share your personal data.

1. Summary

Pixora PDF (“the app”, “we”, “us”) is an Android app published by Umar Farooq that converts images to PDF and provides PDF tools. All processing happens on your device. We do not run servers for the app, we do not have user accounts, and the app does not request the Android internet permission, so it cannot send your files or personal data anywhere.

2. Information we collect

We do not collect, receive or store any personal information, including your name, email address, location, contacts, device identifiers, photos or documents. The app contains no advertising, no analytics and no crash-reporting SDKs.

3. Information processed on your device

To do its job the app handles the following content locally. This content never leaves your device unless you choose to share it (see section 5).

  • Images you select. You choose images with Android’s system photo picker. The app can only read the images you pick.
  • Photos you take with Scan Document. These are saved temporarily in the app’s private cache, used to build your PDF, and removed automatically (files older than about 24 hours are deleted the next time the app starts).
  • PDFs you create. They are saved in Downloads/Pixora PDF or Documents/Pixora PDF (your choice in Settings). You can delete them at any time from the app or your Files app.
  • PDFs you open with the tools (merge, split, rotate, compress, password, PDF to images). The app reads the file you choose and saves the result as a new file. Your original is not changed.
  • Passwords you type for “Add Password” are used in memory only to encrypt the PDF. They are not stored, logged or transmitted. We cannot recover a forgotten password.
  • App preferences such as theme, default page size, default quality, save location and your favourites list are stored locally in the app’s private storage.

4. Permissions

The app requests only the Camera permission, and only to power Scan Document. It does not request access to your photo library, storage, location, microphone, contacts or the internet. See the Permissions page for details.

5. Sharing

The app shares a file only when you tap Share. The file is handed to the app you choose in Android’s share sheet, and that app’s own privacy policy applies. We do not sell or share personal data with third parties.

6. Third-party software

The app uses open-source libraries that run entirely on your device: AndroidX / Jetpack Compose, CameraX, and PdfBox-Android (for PDF processing). They do not transmit data.

7. Google Play

If you have enabled usage and diagnostics sharing on your device, Google may collect technical information about the app (for example crash or performance data) under Google’s own privacy policy. We only see aggregated statistics in Google Play Console and cannot identify individual users.

8. Children

The app is a general-purpose utility and is not directed to children under 13 (or under 16 in the EEA). Because we collect no personal data, no children’s data is collected either.

9. Security

Your files stay on your device and are protected by Android’s app sandbox and storage protections. PDFs you protect with a password are encrypted with AES-128. Keep your password safe; it cannot be reset.

10. Retention and deletion

We hold no data on any server. Data on your device stays until you delete it: delete PDFs in the app (My PDFs) or your Files app, and uninstall the app to remove its settings and cache. Android may keep PDFs you saved in Downloads or Documents after uninstalling, so delete them manually if you no longer want them. See Data Deletion.

11. Your rights

Because we do not collect personal data, there is nothing for us to access, correct, export or erase on your behalf. If you are in the EEA, UK, California or another region with privacy laws and believe we hold information about you, contact us and we will respond promptly.

12. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The effective date at the top shows when it last changed. Material changes will be published on this page and noted in the app’s release notes.
