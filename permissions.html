Permissions Explained

Pixora PDF asks for as little access as possible. Here is exactly what it uses and why.

Permission / accessUsed?Why
Camera
android.permission.CAMERA		Yes, optionalOnly for Scan Document to take photos of pages. If you deny it, every other feature still works.
Photos and videos / storageNo permissionImages are chosen with Android’s system photo picker, which only gives the app the images you select. PDFs are saved with Android’s MediaStore into Downloads/Pixora PDF or Documents/Pixora PDF, which needs no storage permission.
InternetNoThe app cannot connect to the internet.
Location, microphone, contacts, SMS, phoneNoNever requested.

How to change a permission

Open Android Settings → Apps → Pixora PDF → Permissions and switch Camera on or off at any time.
