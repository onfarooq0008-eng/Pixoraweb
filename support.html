Help & Support

Answers to common questions, and how to reach us.

Email: admin@fastvpnn.pp.ua. When reporting a problem, please include your phone model, Android version and what you were doing. Please do not email private documents.

Frequently asked questions

Where are my PDFs saved?

In Downloads/Pixora PDF by default. You can switch to Documents/Pixora PDF in Settings → Save Location. They are listed in the Files tab.

I reinstalled the app and my old PDFs are missing from the Files tab.

Android only lets an app list files created by that installation. Your PDFs are still in Downloads/Pixora PDF; open them with your Files app.

How do I make the PDF smaller?

Choose a lower Image quality in PDF Settings, or use Tools → Compress PDF. Compressed pages become images, so text in the new file can’t be selected.

Why can’t I open or edit a password-protected PDF?

The in-app viewer and tools can’t open encrypted PDFs. Open the file in another PDF app, or use the original unprotected file.

I forgot a PDF password.

We can’t recover it because passwords are never stored. Use the original file if you still have it.

Scan Document shows a black screen or asks for permission.

Allow Camera access in Android Settings → Apps → Pixora PDF → Permissions, then reopen the Scan screen.

Can I reorder pages?

Yes. In Arrange Pages tap Reorder and use the arrows under each page. You can also rotate, crop and delete pages there.

Does the app upload my files?

No. It has no internet permission and works offline.
